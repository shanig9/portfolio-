# ML Engineer & Business Analyst - Cinematic 3D Portfolio

## Project Goals
Create a world-class, immersive portfolio that showcases dual expertise in Machine Learning and Business Analysis through advanced motion design and futuristic aesthetics.

## Key Sections
1. **Hero**: 3D particle background, rotating role titles (ML Engineer, Business Analyst, etc.), mouse-follow glow.
2. **About**: Glassmorphism cards, animated stats, profile area.
3. **Skills**: Interactive 3D skill grid (Python, SQL, ML, Power BI, etc.) with tilt effects.
4. **Experience**: Premium vertical timeline with glowing connectors.
5. **Projects**: "Coming Soon" placeholders with 3D tilt and masonry layout.
6. **Certifications**: Animated carousel of certificate cards.
7. **Contact**: Futuristic form with neon glows and success animations.

## Motion Requirements
- **Atmospheric**: Floating 3D particles, ambient gradients.
- **Interactive**: Scroll-triggered reveals, mouse-tracking light, hover physics.
- **Narrative**: Cinematic text reveals and smooth section transitions.
